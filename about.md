<div class="page-intro">
  <h1>About this catalogue</h1>
  <p>
    This site is intended as a structured catalogue of Cardano performance
    models, especially those expressed in DQSD form, with lighter supporting
    pages for other blockchain systems.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="section-block">
  <h2>Purpose</h2>
  <ul class="section-list">
    <li>Collect models in a consistent format</li>
    <li>Make assumptions and metrics easy to compare</li>
    <li>Preserve a stable academic style across pages</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="section-block">
  <h2>Inclusion notes</h2>
  <p>
    You can use this page to describe your inclusion criteria, notation,
    terminology, and any conventions you use when summarising DQSD models.
  </p>
</div>
